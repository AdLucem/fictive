## Lesson Plan 

The purpose of this lesson plan is to teach concepts from American History using acting pedagogical techniques.

## Session 1: Introducion and Pre-Test

National Core Assessment Standard: TH.Cr.1.1.5.a

_Identify physical qualities that might reveal a character's inner traits in the imagined world of our scenes_

50 minutes

5 min intro. Each time we meet, I'd like a table to meet a table.

Until then- let's create our name plates. 

Students liked the idea of name plates at their desk (like the U.N.)

15-20 min test

Most students expressed they had not learnt the content on the practice test

10 min creative agreement

15-20 min games

### Game 1: Party-Party

Puritan, Farmer, Dissenter, Quaker

Werewolf, Super Hero, Famous Superstar, Teacher

In this game, the students are all assigned roles at a party. Roles are based on historical and fantasy figures.

Create a town meeting about a dissenter... should they be forced to leave the village-

blacksmiths, farmers, merchants, fishermen
