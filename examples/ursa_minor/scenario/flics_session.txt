The purpose of this lesson plan is to teach concepts from American History using acting pedagogical techniques.

## Session 1: Introducion and Pre-Test

National Core Assessment Standard: TH.Cr.1.1.5.a

_Identify physical qualities that might reveal a character's inner traits in the imagined world of our scenes_

50 minutes

5 min intro. Each time we meet, I'd like a table to meet a table. 

[Annotation: the students are seated at separate tables with name plates, so it's like each student is a delegation at the UN]

Until then- let's create our name plates. 

Students liked the idea of name plates at their desk (like the U.N.)

15-20 min test

Most students expressed they had not learnt the content on the practice test

15-20 min games

### Game 1: Party-Party

Puritan, Farmer, Dissenter, Quaker

[Annotation: It's a game in which you give an archetype- described below! - that everyone wants to play, and you tie the archetypes to historical truths i.e: what was it like to live in this time and place? It increases class engagement.]

[Annotation: This is an active exercise that requires physical participation and buy-in from the student. When the student sees a puritan, for instance, they may not know what that means... but if you make them roleplay a provocative archetype... now they're starting to think, oh, puritans lived like this... and they get the idea of t, for example, the puritan character in the body, they're able to think about what it would have felt like, or smelled like.]

Werewolf, Super Hero, Famous Superstar, Teacher

In this game, the students are all assigned roles at a party. Roles are based on historical and fantasy figures.
